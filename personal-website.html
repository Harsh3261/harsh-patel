About

I'm a quality assurance professional with a mechanical engineering foundation — a Bachelor's in Mechanical Engineering from Babaria Institute of Technology in Vadodara, and a Master's in Mechanical Engineering – Automotive from the University of Windsor — and a track record across automotive, robotics and hydraulics manufacturing.

The route here: quality inspector at PIC (a TRIGO company) in Windsor, quality work at Toyota Boshoku Canada in Woodstock, then Avidbots in Kitchener, inspecting autonomous floor-scrubbing robots — and since September 2023, Hydra Dyne Technology in Ingersoll, where I've climbed QA Technician 2 → QA Engineer → Senior QA Engineer and am now stepping into a management role.

Day to day at Hydra Dyne: supplier audits and PPAP on both sides of the fence, CMM programming and first article inspections, the GagePack calibration system, and RMAs, root cause investigations and CAPAs seen through to completion — working alongside assembly, CNC machining, design engineering and supply chain.

The full profile lives on LinkedIn; my code and this site's files live on GitHub.

Current chapter: Hydra Dyne

Where I work now, briefly — and where my quality role sits in it.

Hydra Dyne Technology, in Ingersoll, Ontario, designs and manufactures custom hydraulic components for heavy-duty off-road equipment — hydraulic cylinders, rotary unions and manifold blocks among them.

Where I fit: my quality work runs across that product range — supplier audits and PPAP for the parts coming in, CMM programs and first article inspections for what's machined here, calibration for the gauges that judge it, and prototype PPAP for what's going out to OEM customers. Rotation, pressure and precision machining are the three forces in every part; quality is making sure all three hold at once.
