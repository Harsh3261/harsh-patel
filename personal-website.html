    Current chapter: Hydra Dyne

    Where I work now, briefly — and where my quality role sits in it.

    Hydra Dyne Technology, in Ingersoll, Ontario, designs and manufactures custom hydraulic components for heavy-duty off-road equipment — hydraulic cylinders, rotary unions and manifold blocks among them.

    Where I fit: my quality work runs across that product range — supplier audits and PPAP for the parts coming in, CMM programs and first article inspections for what's machined here, calibration for the gauges that judge it, and prototype PPAP for what's going out to OEM customers. Rotation, pressure and precision machining are the three forces in every part; quality is making sure all three hold at once.
